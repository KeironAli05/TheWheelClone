THE WHEEL - OPTIONAL AUDIO FILES

Save MP3 files in this folder using these exact filenames. Audio is optional; the game works without these files.

player-select.mp3
  Plays when the host selects the next player and the TV runs the name reveal.

spin-start.mp3
  Plays when the host presses the spin-jingle button before spinning the chair.

spin-stop.mp3
  Plays when the chair lands on an expert who is shut down and the turn ends without a question.

question-reveal.mp3
  Plays when the chair lands on an available expert and the question appears.

correct.mp3
  Plays when the host reveals a correct player answer.

incorrect.mp3
  Plays when the host reveals an incorrect player answer, including a wrong final answer.

final-reveal.mp3
  Plays when the Birthday final question begins.

victory.mp3
  Plays when the final answer wins the game.
