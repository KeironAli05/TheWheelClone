# 🎡 The Wheel --- Birthday Party Rules

## The Goal

The aim of the game is simple:

> **One player must successfully answer every category without getting a
> question wrong.**

If a player manages to clear every category, they then face **one final
question about the birthday girl**.

Get that final question right, and **the birthday girl wins her
presents!** 🎁

If they get it wrong, the run ends and the game resets.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. The Setup

There are three main parts to the game:

-   **Experts** --- the people who are knowledgeable about the different
    categories.
-   **Players** --- the guests who choose to take part in the game.
-   **Categories** --- the subjects that players have to work through.

There can be any number of experts and any number of players.

Each expert is associated with a particular category.

For example:

  Expert   Category
  -------- -------------------
  Alex     Football
  Sarah    Music
  Tom      Geography
  Josh     Films
  Emily    Gaming
  Dan      History
  Chloe    General Knowledge

The exact number of categories and experts can be changed depending on
the party.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Choosing a Player

At the beginning of each round, the **Player Wheel** selects one of the
guests who has chosen to participate.

That player comes up to play.

Players do **not** necessarily take turns in a fixed order.

The wheel decides who plays next.

A player can therefore be selected multiple times during the game.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Choosing a Category

The selected player chooses one of the categories currently available on
the wheel.

For example:

> "I'm going for Music."

That category becomes the active category for the question.

Once a category has been successfully answered, it is removed from the
wheel.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Choosing an Expert to Shut Down

Before the main wheel is spun, the player chooses **one expert to shut
down**.

The shut-down expert cannot help with this question.

The player should therefore choose the expert they think is least useful
for the category they have selected.

For example:

> Category: Music\
> Shut down: Geography expert

The shut-down expert is then unavailable for the upcoming spin.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Spin the Main Wheel

The main wheel is spun to select an expert.

There are three possible outcomes.

### The category expert is selected

The expert who specialises in the chosen category is selected.

They help the player with the question.

### Another expert is selected

A different expert is selected.

They still have to help answer the question, even though the question is
outside their speciality.

### The shut-down expert is selected

The wheel lands on the expert who was shut down.

The player **does not get a question**.

Their turn ends immediately and they go back down.

The player wheel is then spun again to select the next player.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Answering the Question

The question is based on the **category chosen by the player**, not
necessarily the category of the expert who was selected.

For example:

> The player chooses Football.

The wheel selects the History expert.

The question is still a **Football question**.

The selected expert discusses the question with the player and helps
them reach an answer.

The player then gives their final answer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. All Experts Answer

While the selected expert is helping the player, **all of the experts
answer the question themselves**.

This means the host can reveal afterwards who got the question right and
who got it wrong.

The selected expert's answer is particularly important because they were
the expert helping the player.

The other experts' answers can also be used for the game's other
mechanics.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. The Player Gets the Question Right

If the player gets the question correct:

-   The category is **cleared**.
-   That category is removed from the wheel.
-   The player returns to the pool of players.
-   The Player Wheel is spun again.

The game continues with fewer categories available.

For example:

> Football ✅\
> Music ❌\
> Geography ❌\
> Films ❌\
> Gaming ❌\
> History ❌\
> General Knowledge ❌

After successfully answering Football:

> Football ~~✅~~ **CLEARED**

Football is no longer available.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. The Player Gets the Question Wrong

This is the big reset.

If the player gets their question wrong:

> **Their entire run is lost.**

All previously cleared categories return to the wheel.

For example, a player might have successfully cleared:

> Football → Music → Geography → Films

Then they get the next question wrong.

Everything resets:

> Football → Music → Geography → Films → Gaming → History → General
> Knowledge

The next player must start again from the beginning.

The goal is therefore for **one player to survive an entire run without
making a mistake**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. An Expert Gets the Question Wrong

The experts are also answering every question.

If an expert gets the question wrong, they become **shut down for the
next spin**.

This can happen even if the player did not choose them as their
shut-down expert.

For example:

> Player shuts down the Geography expert.

The wheel selects the Music expert.

The question is asked.

The Music expert gets it wrong.

The Music expert is now automatically shut down for the next spin as
well.

This means there can potentially be more than one unavailable expert on
the next spin.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. Multiple Shut-Down Experts

If an expert has been automatically shut down because they got a
previous question wrong, they remain unavailable for the next spin.

The player still gets to choose **one additional expert** to shut down.

Therefore, a spin can have multiple experts who cannot be selected.

The wheel only needs to spin among the experts who are currently
available.

Once the relevant turn is over, the temporary shut-downs are cleared
unless the game rules specifically state otherwise.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. Completing All Categories

Eventually, a player may successfully clear every category.

For example:

> Football ✅\
> Music ✅\
> Geography ✅\
> Films ✅\
> Gaming ✅\
> History ✅\
> General Knowledge ✅

At this point, the player has completed the main game.

**But they have not won yet.**

There is one final challenge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. The Birthday Girl Question 🎂

Once all categories have been successfully cleared, the player sees the
accuracy percentage for every expert and chooses one of three options:

-   **Best expert:** the expert with the highest accuracy; answer three
  Birthday questions correctly out of three.
-   **Second-best expert:** the expert ranked second by accuracy; answer
  two Birthday questions correctly out of two.
-   **Worst expert:** the expert with the lowest accuracy; answer one
  Birthday question correctly.

The chosen expert answers each question and helps the player before the
host locks in the player's answer. Every question in the chosen challenge
must be answered correctly to win. The Birthday question pool must have
at least as many unique questions as the selected challenge requires.

This question is separate from the normal categories.

It should be something that tests how well the players know her.

Examples:

-   What is her favourite film?
-   What was her childhood nickname?
-   What is her favourite food?
-   Where did she go on holiday last year?
-   What is her biggest pet peeve?
-   What was the name of her first pet?
-   What is her favourite artist?
-   What would she choose if she could only eat one meal forever?

The exact question should be decided by the host.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 14. Winning the Game 🎁

The player wins the game if they:

1.  Successfully clear **every category**.
2.  Correctly answer the **final birthday girl question**.

If they do both:

# 🎉 THE BIRTHDAY GIRL GETS HER PRESENTS! 🎁

The game is over.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 15. Failing the Final Question

If the player gets any Birthday question in their chosen challenge
wrong:

> **They fail the run.**

The categories are reset.

The Player Wheel is spun again.

Another player gets the opportunity to attempt the full run.

The birthday girl does **not** get her presents until somebody
successfully completes the entire challenge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 16. The Complete Game Loop

The overall game therefore looks like this:

**Player Wheel**

↓

**Player selected**

↓

**Player chooses a category**

↓

**Player chooses an expert to shut down**

↓

**Main Wheel spins**

↓

**Is the selected expert shut down?**

-   **Yes** → Player's turn ends → Spin Player Wheel
-   **No** → Continue

↓

**Question is asked**

↓

**Player and experts answer**

↓

**Did the player get it right?**

### Yes

→ Category is cleared\
→ Check whether all categories are cleared

**If categories remain:**

→ Spin Player Wheel

**If all categories are cleared:**

→ Ask the final Birthday Girl question

→ Correct = **GAME WON 🎁**

→ Wrong = **RESET AND START AGAIN**

### No

→ Reset all cleared categories\
→ Spin Player Wheel\
→ New run begins

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 17. What Makes the Game Difficult

The main challenge is not simply knowing the answers.

Players have to make decisions about:

-   Which category they want to attempt.
-   Which expert they want to shut down.
-   Whether the expert selected by the wheel is likely to be useful.
-   Whether they trust their own answer.
-   How long they can maintain a perfect run.

A player can be **one category away from victory** and still lose the
entire run with one wrong answer.

That is the point of the game.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 18. The Golden Rule

There is one rule that matters above everything else:

> ## One player. Every category. No mistakes. Then one final question about the birthday girl.

**Get everything right → birthday presents. 🎁**

**Get anything wrong → back to square one.**
